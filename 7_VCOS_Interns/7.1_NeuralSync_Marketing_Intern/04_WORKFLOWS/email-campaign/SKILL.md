# NeuralSync email activation

## Use when

The user asks to audit email readiness, select an ICP, critique a proposal, build a message map, draft a sequence, or prepare a campaign for approval.

## Do not use to

Send, schedule, upload lists, enrich contacts, change CRM, configure domains, authenticate tools, buy credits, or publish.

## Required inputs

- campaign objective and type;
- proposed ICP and workflow;
- source-of-truth documents;
- list provenance/jurisdictions;
- offer/CTA and reply owner;
- proof assets and evidence states;
- current stack inventory;
- success and safety thresholds.

If missing, create a gap register and continue with the parts that can be completed safely.

## Workflow

1. Classify every source as confirmed, proposed, unverified, or historical.
2. Audit stack, permissions, data flow, cost, deliverability, and compliance.
3. Score candidate segments and select one recommendation.
4. Translate ICP pain into a message map without inventing claims.
5. Build a claim ledger and select evidence-safe proof.
6. Draft a 3–5 touch sequence with one purpose per touch.
7. Define experiment, metrics, stop rules, and reply handling.
8. Run the QA checklist.
9. Produce an approval packet and stop at `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`.

## Output

Use the structure in `WORKFLOW.md` and include every unresolved decision. Do not hide uncertainty inside polished copy.
