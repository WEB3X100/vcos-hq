# Email approval-packet template

## 1. Decision requested

State exactly what humans are being asked to approve and what remains excluded.

## 2. Source register

| Source | Status | Used for | Conflicts/limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 3. Stack audit

Inventory platform, owner, purpose, data read/write, auth, cost, health, portability, and risk. Record unknowns rather than guessing.

## 4. ICP recommendation

Show scoring, chosen segment, chosen workflow, trigger, disqualifiers, and why alternatives were deferred.

## 5. Offer and CTA

Use one offer and one next step. Flag booking-link, duration, capacity, or commercial gaps.

## 6. Message map

| Buyer language | Observed symptom | Workflow hypothesis | Evidence | Safe claim | CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## 7. Draft sequence

For each touch include objective, subject, body, CTA, personalization input, claim IDs, and stop condition.

## 8. Experiment design

Define cohort, sample, variable, primary metric, guardrails, review date, and decision rule.

## 9. Reply operations

Define positive, objection, unsubscribe, wrong-person, referral, complaint, and out-of-office handling with owners and SLA.

## 10. Approval table

| Item | Owner | Status | Evidence/link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

Final status may be `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`, never “launched” or “approved” without recorded approval.
