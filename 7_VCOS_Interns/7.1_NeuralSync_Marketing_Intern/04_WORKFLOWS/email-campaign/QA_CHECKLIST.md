# Email campaign QA checklist

- [ ] One ICP, workflow, offer, CTA, and sender identity.
- [ ] Campaign type is explicit.
- [ ] List provenance, jurisdiction, suppression, and unsubscribe handling documented.
- [ ] Domain/inbox/authentication status verified by the owner.
- [ ] Every material claim has a source and evidence state.
- [ ] No fabricated logo, quote, testimonial, metric, or client outcome.
- [ ] DEMO/PROTOTYPE/LIVE/CLIENT-APPROVED labels are accurate.
- [ ] Copy does not imply replacement, guarantee, or full autonomy.
- [ ] Personalization uses approved fields and avoids sensitive data.
- [ ] Reply owner, SLA, and escalation path named.
- [ ] Metrics and stop rules are approved.
- [ ] No list upload, send, scheduling, CRM write, spend, or tool connection occurred.
- [ ] Final status is `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`.
