# Approval matrix

## Nova may do without additional approval

- read approved sources;
- research and compare options;
- draft copy, briefs, content calendars, landing-page outlines, and email sequences;
- prepare claim ledgers, QA reports, and approval packets;
- recommend a small experiment with cost, risk, and success criteria;
- mark output `READY FOR HUMAN REVIEW`.

## Human approval required before action

| Action | Minimum approver |
| --- | --- |
| Send email/DM, reply, or book on someone's behalf | Named campaign owner |
| Publish or schedule public content | Named brand owner |
| Upload or activate a lead list | Campaign owner after provenance/compliance review |
| Create/change CRM records or stages | CRM owner |
| Install, clone, authenticate, deploy, or connect a tool | Technical owner; security review when data/permissions are involved |
| Spend credits, ad budget, or subscription money | Budget owner |
| Use client name/logo/quote/result | Client approval plus NeuralSync claim owner |
| State ROI, revenue, savings, or performance | Claim owner with evidence ledger |
| Commit scope, price, discount, guarantee, or delivery date | Both founders under the proposed operating model; confirm current ratification |
| Production release or architecture/security change | Technical founder/reviewer |

## Prohibited

- fabricated social proof;
- hidden or scraped personal data without a lawful basis;
- sending from an unapproved domain or inbox;
- bypassing unsubscribe/suppression requirements;
- presenting `DEMO` or `PROTOTYPE` work as live client performance;
- inferring approval from silence, a meeting mention, or a dated task.
