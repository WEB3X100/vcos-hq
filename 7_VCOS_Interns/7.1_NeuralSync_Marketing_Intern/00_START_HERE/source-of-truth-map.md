# Source-of-truth map

## Precedence

When sources disagree, use this order:

1. explicit current founder approval recorded in `06_DECISIONS/decision-log.md`;
2. current files in this intern workspace;
3. ratified company policy and approved client agreements;
4. approved Drive assets linked in `07_REFERENCES/external-source-map.md`;
5. current project state in `05_PROJECTS/`;
6. meeting notes, draft decks, proposals, keyword files, and screenshots;
7. historical files.

Lower-level sources may inspire a hypothesis but cannot override a higher-level rule.

## Information classes

| Class | Meaning | May appear publicly? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `CONFIRMED` | Current approved fact or rule | Yes, subject to claim review |
| `PROPOSED` | Recommendation awaiting approval | No, unless clearly framed as a proposal |
| `UNVERIFIED` | Research lead or claim lacking adequate evidence | No |
| `HISTORICAL` | Former plan, dated assignment, or superseded material | No, except provenance |

## Memory model

- Memory holds short, durable facts and pointers.
- Canonical documents stay in the repo or approved Drive folders.
- Repeatable procedures become skills/workflows.
- Short-lived campaign status stays in `05_PROJECTS/`.
- Team-wide decisions require an approved shared decision record.

## Conflict examples already resolved

- NeuralSync is the only public brand; older dual-brand documents are historical.
- Demo duration is unresolved because old sources say 15 and 20 minutes. Do not publish either.
- The LinkedIn screenshot claiming a surge in “Business OS” interest is an unverified research lead.
- Search-volume rows grouped by Google must not be summed.
- Suggestions to use synthetic testimonials or fake logos are rejected.
