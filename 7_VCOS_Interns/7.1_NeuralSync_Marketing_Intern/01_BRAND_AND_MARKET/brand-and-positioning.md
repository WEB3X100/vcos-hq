# Brand and positioning

Status: current working standard

## Public brand

NeuralSync is the only public-facing brand.

## Category

NeuralSync is an **AI-powered operating system for businesses**: a supervised operating layer that connects approved business context, workflows, tools, and human decisions so people and AI agents can produce reliable work together.

“Business operating system” is positioning language, not a claim that NeuralSync replaces a computer operating system, ERP, CRM, or every tool.

## Problem

Businesses lose context and control when customer conversations, records, policies, tasks, and AI tools live in disconnected systems. The symptoms are missed follow-up, unclear ownership, repeated manual work, blind automation, and leaders acting as the integration layer.

## Promise

NeuralSync helps a business turn an approved workflow into a governed operating loop with shared context, clear ownership, evidence, approval gates, and exception handling.

## Differentiators

- workflow and outcome before automation;
- business-owned context and portable operating assets;
- human control for consequential actions;
- evidence and audit trails before claims;
- start with one painful workflow, prove it, then expand;
- operate alongside existing systems during adoption.

## Delivery model

`Discover → Map → Prove → Operate`

## Evidence vocabulary

- `DEMO`: intended experience using sample or synthetic data.
- `PROTOTYPE`: working controlled implementation.
- `LIVE`: running in a named environment with verified scope.
- `CLIENT-APPROVED`: result, quote, logo, or outcome cleared for external use.

Current public proof ceiling: capabilities and demos. No verified client-outcome claims are approved in this workspace.
