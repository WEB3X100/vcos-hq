## Email

Email is a proposed activation channel, not an authorized send. Separate:

- lifecycle/nurture email to opted-in or existing relationships;
- cold outbound to a lawfully sourced, narrow B2B list.

They require different consent, copy, infrastructure, and measurement.

## Distribution rule

One source insight may become several channel-native assets, but every derivative inherits the same evidence and approval state.
