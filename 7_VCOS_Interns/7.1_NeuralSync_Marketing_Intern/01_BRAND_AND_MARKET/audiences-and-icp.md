# Audiences and ICP

Status: broad ICP confirmed; first email segment still requires founder selection

## Broad ICP

Founder-led or operations-led small and mid-sized businesses that have:

- multiple disconnected tools and communication channels;
- customer inquiries or internal requests that lose context;
- inconsistent follow-up or unclear ownership;
- leaders manually reconnecting information;
- interest in AI but concern about control, reliability, or adoption.

Likely roles: founder/owner, COO/operations leader, revenue/sales leader, and process owner.

## Job to be done

“Help me prove one valuable operating workflow without losing control, disrupting current systems, or buying another black box.”

## Prioritization score

Score each proposed segment 0–2 on:

1. painful repeated workflow;
2. identifiable process owner;
3. measurable baseline;
4. accessible proof/demo;
5. reachable list with lawful provenance;
6. ability to start small;
7. founder delivery capacity.

Do not launch until one segment and one workflow are chosen.

## Current signals, not conclusions

- Dealership workflows are the most developed demo/wedge in current internal materials.
- Real-estate AI terms have search evidence, but that does not make real estate the approved first outbound ICP.
- Restaurant, travel, professional-services, and startup examples are discovery leads only.
- Age, geography, employee count, revenue band, and tech-stack filters remain unapproved.

## Disqualifiers

- no clear workflow pain;
- buyer wants unsupported full autonomy;
- requires public claims or customer proof that NeuralSync cannot substantiate;
- unclear data authority or compliance basis;
- scope cannot be tested safely in a narrow pilot.
