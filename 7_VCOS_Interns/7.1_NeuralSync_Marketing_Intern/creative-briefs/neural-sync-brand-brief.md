# NeuralSync / AgentOSync Brand Brief

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Lane | AgentOSync public acquisition |
| ICP (primary) | SMB owners — real estate agencies, barbershops, dealerships, clinics |
| ICP (secondary) | Ops managers drowning in missed calls and manual follow-up |
| Offer | AI voice agent + workflow automation — answers instantly, books, hands off |
| Funnel stage | Awareness → curiosity → demo request |
| Platform | LinkedIn vertical video (9:16), 15–60s |

## Villain

Phone tag. Voicemail. The 6-hour callback. Leads calling competitors while your team is "busy."

## Hero

AgentOSync AI employee: picks up in seconds, qualifies, books, logs to CRM, sends handoff packet.

## Feeling

Incredulous disbelief → "wait, you're still doing THAT?" → relief → urgency to not get left behind.

## Visual Language

- Dark backgrounds, high contrast white text (Space Grotesk Bold energy)
- Red/glitch for pain scenes; green glow for salvation/UI scenes
- Camera shake on hook reveals
- Data viz for stat moments — not stock-photo smiles

## Hook Formulas (approved patterns)

1. **Stat flash:** "What if I told you [ICP] loses $X/year on phone tag?"
2. **Incredulous:** "Wait… you're telling me [absurd current behavior]?"
3. **Competitor contrast:** "They called your competitor. Their AI picked up in 8 seconds."
4. **Pattern break:** Burning cash, missed-call counter, voicemail graveyard visual

## Claim Risk Flags

| Claim | Risk | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "$235K/year" real estate loss | Medium | Use as illustrative; add "industry estimates" in caption if published |
| "87% go to voicemail" | High | Source or soften to "most" |
| "8 second pickup" | Low | Product capability if demo proves it |
| Client logos | Blocked | No logos without written approval |

## CTA Stack

1. Primary: "Book a 15-min live demo" / DM "DEMO"
2. Secondary: Link in bio → AgentOSync landing
3. Tertiary: Comment keyword trigger for MVP funnel (future)
