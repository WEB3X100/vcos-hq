# Claims and approvals

## Evidence states

- `DEMO`: intended experience using sample/synthetic data.
- `PROTOTYPE`: controlled working implementation.
- `LIVE`: running in a named environment with verified scope.
- `CLIENT-APPROVED`: cleared for external use.

## Do not claim

- guaranteed ROI, revenue, savings, speed, or accuracy;
- customer outcomes without a client-approved source;
- that NeuralSync replaces every system or employee;
- that a keyword screenshot proves market demand;
- that a dated task or meeting plan is complete;
- that a list, domain, inbox, or sending platform is compliant or healthy without audit.

## Human gates

Stop before sending, publishing, list upload, CRM mutation, auth/permissions, installs/deployments, spend, client-logo use, pricing, scope commitments, or production changes.

Each approval packet must name the decision, approver, evidence, risks, rollback/stop rule, and actions still excluded.
