# AgentOSync Brand Fit

## Core Positioning

AgentOSync builds AI department employees for SMBs.

The brand should feel:

- practical
- operational
- credible
- calm
- founder-led
- useful before flashy
- built for real daily work

## Outcome Language

Prefer:

- recover missed opportunities
- reduce lead wastage
- speed up follow-up
- make daily work visible
- turn scattered work into a control tower
- draft first, human approves
- prove the workflow internally before scaling

Avoid:

- "fully autonomous" unless the workflow is actually trusted and approved
- "guaranteed revenue"
- "replace your team"
- "set it and forget it"
- "AI magic"
- vague future-of-work language with no operational workflow

## Lane-Specific Fit

### Amax

Use for internal proof and operations clarity.

Good assets:

- AR/AP control visuals
- Systrix/GDS/Odoo/Airtable/n8n workflow maps
- daily control prompt examples
- internal training drops

Avoid public case-study language unless approved.

### Dealership

Use as the first public wedge.

Core story:

`A Saturday night missed call becomes a Monday-ready recovery brief, with draft response, test-drive request, CRM update draft, and human approval before anything external happens.`

Good assets:

- Sunday Ghost Town
- missed-call recovery
- Monday Recovery Brief
- test-drive follow-up drafts

Avoid:

- finance/trade-in guarantees
- naming real dealerships
- implying automatic sends

### Ammar

Use for credibility surfaces.

Good assets:

- Upwork portfolio covers
- LinkedIn featured images
- workflow diagrams
- practical proof visuals

Avoid hype. Make it feel employable and trustworthy.

### AgentOSync General

Good assets:

- AI employee operating system
- free department employees
- custom skills as upsell
- proof from daily workflows

Avoid overloading visuals with every vertical at once.
