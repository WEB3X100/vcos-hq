# Media Review Rubric

Use this for every AgentOSync media review.

## Verdicts

| Verdict | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `approved-internal` | Safe for internal review, training, or team discussion. |
| `revise-before-use` | Direction is useful, but needs fixes before use. |
| `blocked-for-public` | Do not publish, send, or show externally. |
| `approved-for-human-review` | Ready for Umair to decide; still not approved to publish. |

## Scoring

Score each category 1-10.

### Hook Clarity

Pass: viewer understands the problem or promise in 2 seconds.

Fail:

- clever but unclear
- too much text
- no visible business problem
- slow setup

### Brand Fit

Pass: practical, credible, operational, founder-led, useful to SMBs.

Fail:

- generic AI hype
- over-polished fake enterprise SaaS
- childish or meme-heavy
- too abstract to connect to a business use case

### Visual Credibility

Pass: looks like a real workflow, real business environment, or polished proof asset.

Fail:

- warped UI
- unreadable text
- fake dashboards
- uncanny people
- stock-photo energy

### Offer Accuracy

Pass: matches what AgentOSync can actually do or is testing.

Fail:

- implies full automation when approval is required
- claims guaranteed revenue
- claims live CRM mutation without approval
- overstates tool integration

### Approval-Boundary Safety

Pass: makes draft-only and human approval clear where needed.

Fail:

- customer message appears sent automatically
- CRM appears updated automatically
- appointment appears confirmed without human review
- client/private data is visible

### Platform Fit

Pass: format matches platform behavior.

Fail:

- mobile text too small
- wrong aspect ratio
- too dense for feed
- too slow for short-form

## Overall Score Bands

- 8.5-10: strong, ready for human review.
- 7.0-8.4: promising, revise before external use.
- 5.0-6.9: useful learning asset, not public-ready.
- below 5.0: reject and rewrite from brief.

## Four Hook Commandments

1. Alignment: hook, visual, and offer say the same thing.
2. Speed to Value: viewer gets the point fast.
3. Clarity: no decoding required.
4. Curiosity: creates enough tension to keep watching or reading.
