# Posting Fit

Use this when deciding where a generated asset belongs in the content calendar.

## Channel Defaults

| Channel | Best use | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LinkedIn | Founder proof, use cases, operational lessons, client-safe narratives | 9:16 video, 1:1 image, or text + visual |
| X | Sharp insights, build-in-public, quick lessons, threads | Text, 1:1 image, short clip |
| Instagram | Visual proof, carousels, behind-the-scenes, short practical reels | 9:16 video, 4:5 carousel |
| Upwork | Trust-building portfolio visuals and project covers | 16:9 still image |
| Website | Proof sections, hero/support visuals, workflow diagrams | 16:9 or responsive stills |
| Internal training | SOPs, checklists, workflow explainers | 16:9 slides, simple cards |

## Weekly Content Mix

Recommended AgentOSync rhythm:

- 2 proof/use-case posts.
- 1 founder/build-in-public post.
- 1 educational teardown.
- 1 offer/CTA post.

Do not publish every generated asset. Most outputs should become either revision fuel or internal examples.

## Posting Decision

Classify the asset:

- `proof`: shows something built or tested.
- `education`: teaches a first-principles lesson.
- `offer`: points to Employee 01, custom skills, or consultation.
- `behind-the-scenes`: shows process, not a finished claim.
- `internal-only`: useful but not public-safe.

## Platform Fit Checks

LinkedIn:

- clear business lesson
- not too meme-like
- CTA should be soft unless proof is strong

X:

- sharper point
- can be rougher or more iterative
- good for threads from a build log

Instagram:

- visual must carry more of the meaning
- avoid dense text
- stronger hook frame needed

Upwork:

- must feel competent and service-ready
- avoid experimental language
- focus on buyer outcomes

## Timing

Default:

- Strong proof: Tue-Thu morning.
- Founder/build-in-public: Mon or Fri.
- Educational teardown: Tue or Wed.
- Offer/CTA: after a proof post, not before.

If the asset is tied to Amax or client-sensitive work, keep it internal until explicitly approved and sanitized.
