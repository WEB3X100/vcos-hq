# NeuralSync Slide Deck Generation

## Required inputs

- audience, decision, presentation setting, and desired next step;
- deck type and target length;
- approved facts, proof, charts, assets, and confidentiality level;
- approved slide master and brand packet from `neuralsync-brand-kit`.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/deck-contract.md` and the Drive manifest.
2. Write a one-sentence decision thesis and a slide-by-slide story outline before layout.
3. Build a source ledger for claims, figures, logos, screenshots, and client information.
4. Resolve each slide to one job and one clear takeaway. Prefer evidence and diagrams over dense prose.
5. Use the approved master, fonts, colors, logo rules, and spacing tokens. Never approximate the brand from memory.
6. Label unresolved material `[HUMAN INPUT REQUIRED]`; never fabricate proof or pricing.
7. Produce editable PPTX and matching PDF.
8. Open both outputs; inspect every slide/page for overlap, clipping, broken fonts, unreadable charts, incorrect aspect ratio, inconsistent numbering, and export drift.
9. Return both files, source ledger, QA report, and approval request. Do not distribute them.

## Completion

A deck draft is complete when its story and sources pass review. The skill is production-accepted only after two real briefs generate PPTX and PDF outputs that open without errors and pass human visual review.
