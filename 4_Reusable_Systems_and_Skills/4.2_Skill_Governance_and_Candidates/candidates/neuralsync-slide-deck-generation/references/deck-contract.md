# Deck Contract and Test Briefs

## Common deck types

- Pitch: problem, stakes, new operating model, solution, proof, engagement, next step.
- Audit: objective, method, observations, evidence, implications, priorities, roadmap, next step.
- Client update: objectives, completed work, evidence, risks, decisions, next actions.
- Portfolio: positioning, capability pillars, selected work, delivery system, proof limits, CTA.

## Test brief A — Chrome Auto proposal deck

- Audience: client decision-makers.
- Sources: Chrome Auto × NeuralSync document and approved pricing input.
- Decision: whether to proceed to a scoped engagement.
- Gate: client facts, scope, pricing, and confidentiality must be approved before rendering.

## Test brief B — NeuralSync portfolio deck

- Audience: prospective operating partners and clients.
- Sources: portfolio draft v4 and internal-review master deck.
- Decision: whether to request a NeuralSync operating-system demo.
- Gate: master, proof claims, and public-safe examples must be approved before rendering.

## QA evidence

For each brief retain the PPTX, PDF, source ledger, slide/page screenshots or inspection notes, and the human decision. A schema-only dry run is not a production pass.
