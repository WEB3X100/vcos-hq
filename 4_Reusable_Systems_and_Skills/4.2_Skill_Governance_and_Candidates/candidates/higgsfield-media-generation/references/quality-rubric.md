# Quality Rubric

Reviewer/QA uses this before generation. Do not spend Higgsfield credits on public-facing assets until the prompt passes.

## Four Hook Commandments

| Commandment | Pass Criteria |
| --- | --- |
| Alignment | Visual, text, audience, and funnel stage all point to the same idea. |
| Speed to Value | SMB viewer can understand the core value within 1-3 seconds. |
| Clarity | The viewer cannot reasonably misunderstand what they are seeing. |
| Curiosity | The asset creates a useful contrast between current pain and new reality. |

## Additional Checks

- Text legibility: on-screen text is short, readable, and not fighting the background.
- Claim safety: every claim is sourced, internal-only, or approval-gated.
- Brand fit: the scene feels credible for AgentOSync/Open Claw, not generic AI stock art.
- Tool fit: image prompts route to Nano Banana Pro; short clips route to Seedance 2.0.
- Product fidelity: reference products, packaging, labels, colors, logos, and proportions are preserved when product identity matters.
- Tracker readiness: batch assets have a row/status/result plan before generation.
- Approval gate: the prompt clearly states whether generation, publishing, or external send is approved.

## Automatic Fail Conditions

- Uses public client proof without approval.
- Includes fake metrics, fake dashboards, fake testimonials, or invented logos.
- Requests public publishing before human approval.
- Uses vague direction such as "make it viral" without scene detail.
- For video, has no motion direction.
- For image-to-video, asks Seedance to invent an important brand/product identity without a reference.
- For product ads, changes the product label, packaging, logo, or product identity without explicit approval.
- For batch generation, lacks status tracking or would duplicate completed rows.
