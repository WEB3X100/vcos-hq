# Client Story Hooks

Use this reference when diagnosing the slide contents and turning them into a memorable client walkthrough.

## Story Principle

The deck should not say “we have AI.” It should make the client feel: “They understand our day, they found where work is leaking, and they have a practical way to make the team calmer and faster without changing how we already communicate.”

## Slide Claim Formula

For each slide, write:

```text
When [role] is trying to [daily job], [specific breakdown] creates [business cost or anxiety]. AgentOSync fixes this by [plain operating change].
```

Then shorten it into one slide title and one visual proof object.

## Attention Hooks That Stay Professional

Use these as story patterns, not as loud copy.

| Hook | Use When | Example Title |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hidden work | the client does work that never becomes visible | `The work is happening, but the operating record is missing.` |
| Same tool, better system | the client fears onboarding another app | `WhatsApp stays. The workflow becomes structured.` |
| Calm owner | the CEO is chasing updates | `The owner sees exceptions without interrupting the team.` |
| Three people, larger rhythm | small team needs leverage | `A three-person team can run with a larger-store rhythm.` |
| Judgment protected | automation anxiety is high | `Speed improves because judgment is routed, not removed.` |
| Leakage map | money/time is disappearing through handoffs | `The leakage is in handoffs, not effort.` |
| Before/after day | the current day is chaotic | `The same day, with fewer blind spots.` |

## Retention Structure

Use a simple repeated arc:

1. Name the role.
2. Show the day-in-life pressure.
3. Show the broken handoff or missing context.
4. Show the operating layer.
5. Show what the role sees tomorrow morning.

For dealership decks, the three chapters are:

- Operations manager: vehicle intake -> listing readiness -> approval.
- Sales manager: buyer message -> urgency -> safe reply/follow-up.
- CEO / owner: exceptions -> approvals -> daily brief.

## Plain Language Library

Prefer:

- organize the messages into an operating record
- route the right item to the right person
- keep approval points visible
- show stale work before it becomes a missed opportunity
- make the current workflow easier to manage
- help the owner support the team without chasing updates

Avoid:

- revolutionize
- unlock explosive growth
- AI-powered magic
- autonomous revenue machine
- guaranteed sales
- closer language
- internal sales coaching labels

## Hook QA

Before building a slide, ask:

- Can the client recognize their day in this slide?
- Is the pain concrete enough that they would nod?
- Does the proof object make the claim obvious without a presenter?
- Is the copy plain enough to be said in a meeting?
- Would this slide still feel professional if forwarded to the CEO?
