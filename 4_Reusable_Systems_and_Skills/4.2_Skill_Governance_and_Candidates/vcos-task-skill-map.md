## Promotion Meter

Use these statuses:

- `none`: no repeatable SOP yet.
- `candidate`: enough signal to test the task as a repeatable SOP.
- `active`: packaged and available as a skill.
- `deprecated`: no longer preferred.

Promotion rule:

- 1 successful use: document the SOP or source file.
- 2 successful uses: mark as `candidate`.
- 3+ successful uses: package as a skill if it reduces time, mistakes, or context load.
- Any failed run: record root cause before promotion.

## Automation Rule

Automation is justified only when:

- The task recurs on a schedule or obvious event.
- Inputs and outputs are predictable.
- Approval gates are explicit.
- Failure is detectable.
- The routine can be tested without touching client-facing or production systems.
