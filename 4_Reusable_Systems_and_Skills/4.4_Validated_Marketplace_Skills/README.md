# Validated Marketplace Skills

Purpose: hold workflows that have graduated beyond internal skill candidates and are close to client-installable.

## Folders

- `ready-to-package/` — validated internally, needs packaging and client-safe examples.
- `client-tested/` — validated with at least one real or approved client-like deployment.

## Entry Requirements

A skill can move here only when:

- the owning department is clear
- 3+ successful uses are recorded or the workflow has strong cross-client proof
- input and output packets are documented
- approval gates are explicit
- secrets are excluded
- install/use instructions are client-safe
- examples do not expose client/private data
