# Finance Mini Intern Capabilities

Purpose: own accounting, bookkeeping, spend visibility, and finance admin workflows without leaking finance context into unrelated interns.

## Candidate Capabilities

| Capability | Status | Job |
| --- | --- | --- |
| QuickBooks workflow mapping | Pattern | Turn repeated bookkeeping tasks into least-privilege workflow specs |
| Invoice/payment reconciliation | Future candidate | Match payments, invoices, and exceptions for review |
| Spend report packet | Future candidate | Summarize tool/API/ad spend for the human |

## Approval Gates

Human approval is required before credential access, write access, invoice/customer mutation, spend, payment actions, or financial claims.
