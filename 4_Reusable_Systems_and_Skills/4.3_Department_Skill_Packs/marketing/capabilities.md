## Default Marketing Workflow

1. Define ICP, offer, funnel stage, and desired feeling.
2. Create a creative brief.
3. Choose asset route: static visual, short clip, website hero loop, avatar/course video, or tracked creative agency batch.
4. For avatar/course/talking-head videos, create an avatar production packet and run a short dry run before any batch.
5. For edited videos or motion graphics, create a Hyperframes video packet with storyboard, assets, captions, and render QA.
6. Generate prompt packet.
7. Run Reviewer QA for alignment, speed to value, clarity, curiosity, brand fit, authenticity risk, and claim risk.
8. Ask for human approval before spending credits or using public/client assets.
9. Generate or assemble the asset.
10. Record prompt, settings, job ID, output link, status, QA score, and lesson learned.
11. Create the next handoff packet for Sales Ops or Operations when needed.

## Niche Intelligence Workflow

Use `VCOS-Niche-Intelligence-Operator` when Marketing needs public creator or YC/company signals turned into content recipes and Seedance asset briefs.

Default route:

1. Select one lane: AgentOSync/NeuralSync AI OS, AI tools/operators, EAG, Travel AI/Amax, or YC strategic signal.
2. Collect manual inputs first: transcripts/comments for creators or sourced snippets for YC/company signals.
3. Extract recipe, topic gaps, hook formula, visual format, Seedance prompt seed, and claim-risk flags.
4. Umair approves or edits the recipe and prompt.
5. Generate draft Seedance asset only after approval.
6. Log result and lesson learned.
7. Package as a skill candidate only after a full useful cycle.

## Approval Gates

Human approval is required before:

- public posting
- LinkedIn publishing
- sending media to prospects or clients
- using client logos/testimonials
- making public claims
- paid generation at meaningful credit cost
- deploying a public site
- creating or publishing digital twins, voice clones, avatar videos, founder likenesses, or client likenesses
