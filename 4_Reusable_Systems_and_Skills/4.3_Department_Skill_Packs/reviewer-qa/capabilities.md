| Capability | Status | Source | Job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AgentOSync media review | Candidate | `../../4.2_Skill_Governance_and_Candidates/candidates/agentosync-media-review/SKILL.md` | Brand fit, platform fit, posting fit, and revision prompts |
